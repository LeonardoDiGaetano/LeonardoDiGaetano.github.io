Leo Di Gaetano

I am a postdoctoral researcher with dual affiliations at the Institut de Neurosciences de la Timone and the Centre de Physique Théorique, Aix‑Marseille Université, supervised by Andrea Brovelli and Alain Barrat.

My research lies at the intersection of network science and neuroscience, with a strong focus on the theoretical foundations of network science and their application to understanding the organization and dynamics of brain networks.

I earned my PhD in Network and Data Science at Central European University, under the supervision of Federico Battiston, with Michele Starnini as an external advisor.
